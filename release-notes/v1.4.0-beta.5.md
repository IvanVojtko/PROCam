## What's new in ProCam 1.4.0-beta.5

### Faster
- **Starts about a second sooner.** The camera now opens while the app is still loading, and ProCam remembers what your cameras can do instead of checking them at every start. On the Jolla Phone 2 the viewfinder appears after about 1.5 s instead of 2.5–2.8 s.
- **Shutter feedback right when the photo is taken.** The shutter sound and animation now come as soon as the last frame of the photo has been captured, instead of after the frames were saved. They tell you when it's safe to move the phone: indoors that's typically 0.7–0.9 s after you press.
- **Turning the phone no longer restarts the camera.** The photo's orientation is applied when it's saved.
- The shutter animation runs smoothly: the photo's thumbnail is made after it has started.

### Sharper viewfinder
- The viewfinder now shows **1920x1440** instead of 1440x1080. A camera that can't deliver that next to its RAW stream switches back to 1440x1080 by itself.

### Selfies
- **New mirror button** in the front camera's top bar. When it's on, selfies are saved the way the viewfinder shows them (mirrored). When it's off (the default), they're saved the way others see you.

### Choose where photos and videos are saved
- New **"Photos folder"** and **"Videos folder"** options in Settings → Apps → ProCam. Choose "Default" or pick any folder in **Pictures**, **Videos** or on a **memory card**. Photos and videos go straight into the folder you pick, and ProCam's gallery follows it.
- If the folder you picked isn't available (memory card taken out, folder deleted), ProCam saves to the default folder instead, `Pictures/ProCam` or `Videos/ProCam`, and Settings tells you.
- Folders elsewhere, such as Documents, can't be used: the Sailfish OS app sandbox only lets ProCam write to Pictures, Videos and memory cards. To use a new folder, create it in the File Browser first.

### Better photos on other phones
- **Damaged RAW frames are detected.** On some phones, the camera fills only part of each RAW frame. On the Xperia 10 III this made photos come out as two squashed copies on black. ProCam now checks every frame before merging and leaves damaged frames out.
- **Automatic switch to YUV.** If every frame of a photo is damaged, that photo fails with a message saying so. That camera then takes its photos from YUV frames, like cameras without RAW, and its RAW options are hidden. Please take the photo again. ProCam remembers this until the next system update, which may fix the camera.
- Photos on the Jolla Phone 2 are unchanged: none of its RAW frames are affected.

### Diagnostics
- `Pictures/ProCam/procam-capture.log` now records start-up, shutter and photo-processing times, and when a camera's RAW frames were found damaged.

### Known limitations
- In low light, the front camera's viewfinder lags behind by up to half a second. This comes from keeping full-resolution selfie frames ready for the photo, and it's kept on purpose for image quality.
- The Xperia 10 III switch to YUV photos hasn't been confirmed on an Xperia yet. Reports are welcome.
- Video: no zoom in the Video tab, no tap-to-focus while recording, and 30 fps only. The Jolla Phone 2's camera driver has no 60 fps mode.
