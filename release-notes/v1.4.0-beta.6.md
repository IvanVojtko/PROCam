## What's new in ProCam 1.4.0-beta.6

### HEIC photos
- New **"Photo format"** option in Settings → Apps → ProCam: **JPEG** (the default) or **HEIC**. HEIC photos are made by the phone's hardware encoder and take about a third less space than JPEG at the same quality.
- In RAW mode the RAW file is kept next to the photo, which is then saved in the format you chose. The Pro top bar shows RAW, JPG or HEIC.
- Every HEIC keeps the photo's details: when it was taken (with time zone), where (when "Save location" is on), its size and orientation, and the camera and exposure settings. If a HEIC can't be saved with them, the photo stays a JPEG and ProCam tells you.
- ProCam installs a HEIC image plugin, since Sailfish OS has none, and a rule that tells the photo indexer that HEIC files are pictures.

### Pro mode, redesigned
- Pro keeps Auto's controls (last photo, shutter, lens) and adds one row of chips above them: **EV, shutter, ISO and focus**. A chip lights up while you set it by hand. Tap it to open its dial.
- The focus dial goes from AF and continuous AF through manual distances to infinity. **Peaking** and **zebra** are at the start of the dial.
- RAW/JPG is a chip in the top bar, and the histogram is a small card under it. Pro now covers about a fifth of the screen instead of 40%.
- In JPEG mode the photo size picker offers the sizes ProCam really renders.

### Flash and torch
- **Flash chip** in the top bar: off (the default), auto or on, on cameras with a flash. A flash photo is a single frame, never an HDR burst. Night mode never flashes.
- **Torch chip** in video. You can turn it on and off while recording.

### Zoom
- **2x is really 2x.** Zoomed photos and the viewfinder were cropped twice, so 2x looked like 4x. Zoom presets are now 1x, 2x and 4x.

### Focus
- **Tap to lock focus.** A tap on the photo viewfinder locks focus there until you tap the "AF locked" chip. In video, where a tap sets brightness, a long press locks focus, and the lock stays while recording.
- **Sharper photos after a tap, especially at night.** ProCam now waits (up to 2 s) for the camera to finish focusing before it takes the photo. Before, a photo taken right after a tap could freeze the lens mid-focus and come out soft, and later photos kept that wrong focus.

### Learned denoise (experimental)
- New **"Learned denoise"** option, off by default. The noisiest HDR and night photos are cleaned up by a small neural network on the phone's graphics processor instead of the standard noise filter. It keeps more texture in dim light and adds about half a second per photo. Brighter photos keep the standard filter.

### Photo details
- Photo viewers now show the right focal length (some showed "-0.0 mm") and exposure times like 1/15 s. Photos are marked as sRGB, and "Software" names the real ProCam version. This applies to JPEG and HEIC.

### Xperia 10 III and other phones
- **YUV photos are no longer hazy.** These are photos from the front and ultra-wide cameras, and from cameras whose RAW is damaged. ProCam now measures how the camera brightens its frames and combines them the same way. A first frame taken at the wrong exposure is left out.
- **Another try before giving up on RAW.** When a camera's RAW frames come out damaged, ProCam first retries RAW with the viewfinder limited to 30 frames per second. The likely cause on the Xperia 10 III is a fast, reduced-resolution sensor mode. Only if RAW is still damaged does that camera switch to YUV photos.
- **Damaged RAW frames are kept for troubleshooting**, even with RAW saving off. They're saved as `ProCam_<time>_damaged.raw16` with a `.json` next to your photos.
- New **"Keep trying RAW"** option for testers: a camera with damaged RAW stays with RAW instead of switching to YUV photos.

### Known limitations
- **The Gallery app doesn't read the date of HEIC photos yet**, so it may list them out of order or not show them. Choose JPEG if you rely on the Gallery. A fix is planned.
- Learned denoise rarely kicks in for night photos: 16-frame night photos are usually clean enough for the standard filter.
- **Not yet confirmed on a phone:**
  - the focus fix after a tap;
  - learned denoise on a real photo in the app;
  - the photo details in HEIC photos with location on;
  - on the Xperia 10 III, the RAW retry at 30 fps and "Keep trying RAW".

  Reports are welcome.
- In very dark scenes the camera can still report focus as found when it isn't. If a night photo comes out soft, tap to focus first.
- In low light, the front camera's viewfinder lags behind by up to half a second. This comes from keeping full-resolution selfie frames ready for the photo, and it's kept on purpose for image quality.
