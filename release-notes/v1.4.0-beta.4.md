## What's new in ProCam 1.4.0-beta.4

### Video: ProCam's own recorder
- **New video recorder, on by default.** Video is now recorded through ProCam's own camera session instead of the system camera service.
- **Stabilization out of the box.** Electronic stabilization works without any system configuration file. A new button in the Video tab's top bar turns it on or off (on by default). The viewfinder shows exactly the stabilised frame that is recorded.
- **Sharper, cleaner video.** The camera's own video noise reduction and sharpening are used, and the encoder records at a constant bitrate with its higher-quality profile, so busy or dim scenes no longer turn blocky and grainy.
- **Choose the size and codec** in the Video tab's menu:
  - **Size:** 1080p or 1440p (2560x1440) at 30 fps. ProCam offers only the sizes both the camera and the phone's video encoder support. On the Jolla Phone 2 that tops out at 1440p, so there is no 4K. Phones with a 4K encoder get a 4K option.
  - **Codec:** H.264 (plays everywhere) or HEVC (smaller files).
- **Instant start and stop.** Recording starts without the viewfinder blinking, and stopping waits until the last frames are in the file.
- **Recordings are never lost.** If you leave the app while recording, the recording keeps going and stops after a minute. If the camera has to close mid-recording, the file is still finished properly.
- **16:9 viewfinder** in the Video tab.
- **Sound** is recorded in sync with the picture. The microphone is only open while you record.
- **Videos are saved to `Videos/ProCam`.**
- **Fallback.** If a phone's camera can't run the new recorder, ProCam switches to the system recorder for that camera automatically. You can also turn the new recorder off in Settings → Apps → ProCam → "ProCam video recorder".
- With the system recorder, the video menu keeps its old entries. If your system configures stabilization for it, that now also applies when ProCam starts directly in the Video tab.

### Photos
- **HDR and night bursts** drop frames whose exposure differs from what was asked for. Some phones (Xperia 10 III) deliver the first frames of a burst with the viewfinder's exposure, which made merged photos uneven. Photos on the Jolla Phone 2 are unchanged.
- **If HDR or night bursts fail on a camera**, ProCam takes normal photos with that camera for the rest of the session instead of failing every shot.
- **Clear message when the photo folder can't be written:** "Cannot write to folder …" instead of a failed capture.

### Diagnostics
- More detail in `Pictures/ProCam/procam-capture.log` when a capture or a recording fails. Every recording is logged with its size, codec, frame rate and bitrate.
- The camera probe lists each camera's stabilization modes.

### Known limitations
- No zoom in the Video tab yet.
- Video uses continuous autofocus; tap-to-focus is not available while recording.
- Video is 30 fps only.
