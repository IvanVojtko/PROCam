## What's new in ProCam 1.4.0-beta.8

### Night photos on the Jolla Phone 2
- **Tripod and table shots now get their long frames.** The main camera's sensor driver can't expose longer than about 0.68 s, and longer requests silently ran much shorter: a "1 s" frame in beta.7 was really 0.32 s. Night shots on a tripod or a table now take 15 frames of 0.66 s each: twice the light per frame of beta.7 and three times the light in all (about 10 s).
- **Pro mode's shutter now stops at 1/2 s** on the main camera. The longer settings (1 s to 16 s) came out shorter than shown.

### Look
- **New launcher icon** in the Sailfish OS style, by SpecSierra.
