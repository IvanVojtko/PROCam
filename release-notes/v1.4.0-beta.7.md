## What's new in ProCam 1.4.0-beta.7

### QR codes
- Point the camera at a QR code and its link (or text) appears in a chip under it. Tap the chip to open the link in the browser; other text is shown with a Copy button. Nothing opens without that tap.
- On by default: **"Detect QR codes"** in Settings → Apps → ProCam.

### Document scanning
- Hold the phone over a page, receipt or card with the camera pointing down, and the viewfinder outlines it. Tap the outline (or the **"Scan document"** chip) to save a flat, straightened photo of just the document. The photo keeps its details, such as the date and location.
- Documents are only looked for while the camera points down, so windows, doors and pictures on a wall aren't outlined in everyday photos.
- On by default: **"Detect documents"** in Settings → Apps → ProCam.

### Night photos
- **Brighter night photos.** Night mode now brightens dark scenes up to 8× the metered exposure (was 3×). In near darkness it brightens less, so the scene stays dark instead of turning into grain.
- **Cleaner shadows.** An extra light cleanup removes the fine grain left in brightened shadows, such as dark walls and the sky.
- **No more noisy square around small lights.** A lamp, a display or a street light in a dark scene no longer leaves a square of coloured grain around it.
- **Tripod and table shots.** In night mode the gyroscope measures how steady the phone is. When it has been still for a second and nothing moves in the frame, ProCam takes fewer, longer frames (up to 1 s each, about 10 s in all) at a much lower ISO.
- **Longer hand-held frames.** Held steady, night frames can now be up to 1/3 s (was 1/5 s).

### Learned denoise
- **On by default** and no longer experimental. If you turned it off, it stays off.
- **New model**, trained on 78 bursts instead of 52, including night scenes with small bright lights.
- **Knows where the merge had less to work with.** Where moving subjects or flickering lights left fewer frames to combine, it now cleans harder.
- **Single-frame photos** (such as flash photos) use it only up to ISO 150; above that the standard filter does better.

### HDR photos
- **No more bright halo around trees** against fog or an overcast sky, or between bare branches.

### Video
- New **"Bitrate"** option in the video menu: the full rate, ½, ¼ or ⅛, with the size per minute shown. Lower rates make smaller clips for sharing.

### Files
- **New file names:** photos and videos are named like `20261120_153012.jpg` instead of `ProCam_20261120_153012.jpg`. Many photo tools and scripts read the date from this format. Older photos keep their names and still sort correctly in ProCam.
- New **"Save photo details"** option in Settings → Apps → ProCam (on by default). Turn it off and ProCam no longer keeps a `.json` file with the capture details next to each photo. Photos keep their EXIF either way, and "RAW + JSON" files keep their `.json`.
- Single-frame RAW photos no longer leave temporary `.warm.raw16` and `.warm.json` files next to the photo.

### Xperia 10 III and other phones
- Cameras that can run faster than 30 frames per second now take photos at 30 fps from the start, instead of only after damaged RAW frames appeared. On the Xperia 10 III this keeps RAW frames intact and burst exposures on time. The Jolla Phone 2 is unchanged.
- If RAW frames are damaged even at 30 fps, the camera switches to YUV photos right away instead of retrying.
